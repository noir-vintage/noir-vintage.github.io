The studio

Classic mood.
Curious spirit.

Noir Vintage Studio is an independent game studio drawn to rich atmosphere, distinctive worlds, and the simple pleasure of play.

We make games that feel like stepping into another place.

Every project begins with a mood, a tiny spark, or a question worth following. We shape that first idea into a playful experience with its own look, rhythm, and sense of discovery.

Our name brings together two things we love: the dramatic charm of vintage storytelling and the bright possibility of games made today. The result is a studio built for curious players and memorable escapes.
